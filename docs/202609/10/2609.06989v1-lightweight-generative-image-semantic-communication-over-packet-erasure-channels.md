## 摘要
本文旨在解决网络拥塞或信道波动导致的语义通信中分组丢失问题。我们提出了 LGSemCom，这是一种用于分组丢失信道下高效且鲁棒的图像传输的轻量级生成式分组级联合信源信道编码（JSCC）框架。与传统基于失真的恢复方法在丢失区域产生模糊平均值不同，LGSemCom 将分组丢失下的图像恢复重新定义为条件生成任务。通过利用对抗学习，所提出的解码器能够在推理过程中不增加复杂性的前提下合成逼真的细节。该框架的一项关键创新是采用了丢失感知加权（EAW）策略，该策略在优先生成丢失区域的同时，保留了正确接收区域的像素保真度。为确保计算效率，LGSemCom 采用了基于高效长程注意力模块（ELABs）的全卷积编解码器，以低复杂度捕获全局语义依赖关系。大量实验表明，与现有基准相比，LGSemCom 在严重分组丢失情况下实现了更优的感知重建质量，同时推理速度提升了一个数量级。这些特性使得 LGSemCom 非常适合资源受限边缘设备上的延迟敏感型应用。

## Abstract
This paper addresses packet loss in semantic communication caused by network congestion or channel fluctuations. We propose LGSemCom, a lightweight generative packet-level joint source-channel coding (JSCC) framework for efficient and robust image transmission over packet erasure channels. Unlike conventional distortion-oriented recovery methods that yield blurry averages over erased regions, LGSemCom reformulates image recovery under packet erasures as a conditional generative task. By leveraging adversarial learning, the proposed decoder synthesizes plausible details without increasing complexity during inference. A key innovation of our framework is an erasure-aware weighting (EAW) strategy, which prioritizes generation in erased regions while preserving pixel fidelity in correctly received areas. To ensure computational efficiency, LGSemCom employs a fully convolutional codec based on efficient long-range attention blocks (ELABs) that capture global semantic dependencies with low complexity. Extensive experiments show that LGSemCom achieves superior perceptual reconstruction quality compared with existing benchmarks under severe packet loss, while achieving an order of magnitude faster inference. These attributes make LGSemCom highly suitable for latency-sensitive applications on resource-constrained edge devices.
