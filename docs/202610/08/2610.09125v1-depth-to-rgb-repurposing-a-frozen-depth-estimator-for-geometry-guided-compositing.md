## 摘要
基于参考的对象合成利用背景图像、参考图像和二维合成掩码来插入或替换对象。这些输入虽然引导了外观和位置，但使得合成场景的几何结构处于隐式状态，从而可能导致对象结构扭曲或周围环境改变。我们的 Depth-to-RGB (D2R) 框架能够预测 RGB 输入中尚未观测到的场景的合成深度。它通过使用成对完整场景的编码器特征作为目标，学习对冻结的深度估计器进行参考条件下的修正。未改变的解码器将修正后的表示映射为目标场景的深度，而单独训练的渲染器在 RGB 合成过程中保持固定。在匹配的架构和训练条件下，与解码深度监督相比，编码器特征监督将分布外（OOD）阶段一的绝对相对误差（AbsRel）降低了 31.4%。我们还引入了 AnyInsertion++，包含成对的分布内和类别不相交数据集，以评估合成训练类别之外的泛化能力。完整的 D2R 系统在两类成对数据集上的估计器导出几何质量和光度质量方面，均领先于 12 个开源和 3 个闭源基准模型。在类别不相交的数据上，D2R 的 AbsRel 降低了 43.7%，PSNR 较匹配的 RGB 基准提高了 2.4 dB。在三个非成对基准测试中，D2R 在身份指标上均处于领先地位，并将平均 CLIP 参考余弦距离较最强基准降低了 55%。项目页面：https://shjo-april.github.io/Depth2RGB/

## Abstract
Reference-based object compositing inserts or replaces an object using a background image, a reference image, and a 2D compositing mask. These inputs guide appearance and placement but leave the completed scene's geometry implicit, which can distort object structure or alter the surroundings. Our Depth-to-RGB (D2R) framework predicts composite depth for a scene not yet observed in the RGB inputs. It learns reference-conditioned corrections to a frozen depth estimator using encoder features of paired completed scenes as targets. The unchanged decoder maps the corrected representation to the intended scene's depth, which a separately trained renderer holds fixed during RGB synthesis. Under matched architecture and training, encoder-feature supervision reduces OOD Stage-1 AbsRel by 31.4% relative to decoded-depth supervision. We also introduce AnyInsertion++ with paired in-distribution and category-disjoint splits to evaluate generalization beyond compositing training categories. The complete D2R system leads 12 open-source and 3 closed-source baselines in estimator-derived geometry and photometric quality on both paired splits. On category-disjoint data, D2R reduces AbsRel by 43.7% and improves PSNR by 2.4 dB over the matched RGB baseline. Across three unpaired benchmarks, D2R leads both identity metrics and reduces mean CLIP reference cosine distance by 55% relative to the strongest baseline.
