## 摘要
自监督视觉表示学习旨在无需人工标注的情况下学习有用的特征。基于图像的联合嵌入预测架构（I-JEPA）通过预测掩码图像区域的潜在表示进行学习，但其目标函数并未显式建模对特定视觉干预的响应。我们引入了 CI-JEPA，这是一种感知反事实干预的扩展模型，旨在学习预测原始图像与其修改版本之间的表示变化 ΔZ = Z_CF - Z。我们通过选择性敏感度来评估表示的鲁棒性，即对任务相关的语义变化表现出比干扰性变化更强的响应。在 Flowers102 数据集上的实验中，我们将花心遮挡作为候选语义干预，将背景模糊和色调调整作为候选干扰性干预。在冻结编码器的线性探测设置下，CI-JEPA 达到了 78.14% 的最高验证准确率，相比之下，预训练的 ViT-B/16 和 I-JEPA 基准模型均为 77.55%，提升了 0.59 个百分点。实验报告的平均 L2 表示变化分别为：中心遮挡 4.42，背景色调 3.48，背景模糊 2.83。这一排序与评估干预措施的相对语义选择性一致，而非完全的干扰不变性。准确率的比较仅作为补充，并未证明在鲁棒性方面优于基准模型。这些受控的图像修改为研究 JEPA 表示中由干预引起的变化提供了一个框架；它们并不等同于因果特征发现或对所有视觉变化的鲁棒性。

## Abstract
Self-supervised visual representation learning learns useful features without manual annotations during representation training. The image-based joint-embedding predictive architecture (I-JEPA) predicts latent representations of masked image regions, but its objective does not explicitly model responses to specified visual interventions. We introduce CI-JEPA, a counterfactual intervention-aware extension that learns to predict the representation change $ΔZ = Z_{\mathrm{CF}} - Z$ between an original image and a modified counterpart. We assess representation robustness through selective sensitivity: stronger responses to task-relevant semantic changes than to nuisance changes. Experiments on Flowers102 use flower-center occlusion as a candidate semantic intervention and background blur and tint as candidate nuisance interventions. With frozen-encoder linear probing, CI-JEPA achieves a best validation accuracy of 78.14\%, compared with 77.55\% for both the pretrained ViT-B/16 and the I-JEPA baseline, a gain of 0.59 percentage points. The reported mean $L_2$ representation changes are 4.42 for center occlusion, 3.48 for background tint, and 2.83 for background blur. This ordering is consistent with relative semantic selectivity for the evaluated interventions, rather than complete nuisance invariance. The accuracy comparison is complementary and does not establish improved robustness over the baselines. These controlled image modifications provide a framework for studying intervention-induced changes in JEPA representations; they do not establish causal feature discovery or robustness to all visual changes.
