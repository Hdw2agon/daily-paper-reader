## 摘要
与专业医院超声设备相比，低成本手持式超声设备具有广泛部署的潜力。然而，其图像往往存在复合退化问题，可能误导临床判断。基于这一观察，将手持式低质量（LQ）图像映射为医院高质量（HQ）图像已成为一个重要的研究课题。传统方法要求 LQ-HQ 图像对在像素级对齐，但在实际场景中，不同时间拍摄的真实扫描图像无法实现像素级对齐，因此该要求难以满足。本文提出了一个两阶段框架来解决这一挑战。第一阶段生成像素级对齐的 LQ-HQ 数据集，第二阶段训练增强模型以提升 LQ 图像质量。在第一阶段，通过在未对齐的真实 LQ-HQ 图像对上训练循环一致性风格迁移模型，学习 HQ 到 LQ 的映射，进而将真实 HQ 图像转换为像素级对齐的 LQ 图像。在第二阶段，基于第一阶段生成的数据集，利用双重退化引导（DDG）低秩自适应（LoRA）方法，对基于对齐图像对的 LQ 到 HQ 模型进行微调。该阶段模型基于著名的 PiSA-SR 框架，并引入了退化条件校正矩阵。在 USenhance2023 数据集上的实验结果表明，该方法的 FID 指标较最强基线提升了 16.7%，其他指标也表明增强后的输出与真实 HQ 分布高度一致。本研究的源代码已开源至 https://github.com/Jason0411202/DDG_LoRA。

## Abstract
Low-cost handheld ultrasound devices can be widely deployed compared to professional hospital ultrasound machines. However, their images suffer from compound degradation that can mislead clinical judgment. Motivated by this observation, mapping handheld low-quality (LQ) to hospital high-quality (HQ) images has been considered a valuable research question. Conventionally, the mapping requires pixel-aligned LQ-HQ pairs. This requirement is unsatisfactory in practical scenarios because real scans at different times are never pixel-aligned. This paper addresses the challenge with a two-stage framework. The first stage generates pixel-aligned LQ-HQ datasets, and the second stage trains an enhancement model that improves LQ images. The first stage trains a cycle-consistent style-transfer model on unaligned real LQ-HQ pairs to learn a HQ-to-LQ model. Then, the model transforms real HQ images into pixel-aligned LQ images. Based on the dataset generated by the first stage, the second stage uses the Dual Degradation-Guided (DDG) Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) method to fine-tune an LQ-to-HQ model based on aligned pairs. In this stage, the model is based on the well known PiSA-SR framework but inserts a degradation-conditioned correction matrix. Experimental results on the USenhance2023 dataset show that the FID metric is improved by 16.7% over the strongest baseline while other metrics indicate that our enhanced outputs are well aligned with the real HQ distribution. The source code of our method is available at https://github.com/Jason0411202/DDG_LoRA.
